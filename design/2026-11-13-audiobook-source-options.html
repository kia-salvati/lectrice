Play from an audiobook file: three options

A book can be listened to from an M4B file instead of speech made on the phone. The three options below differ only in where you switch between the two: A puts a switch in the player sheet, B opens a small sheet from one row, C puts a chip on the pill. The screens under “Shared by all options” are the same whichever is picked.

Every screen is shown in light and dark, in the approved Quiet v2 look. All names and text are placeholders (book “The Long Book”, 14 chapters, voice Heart). Spec: docs/android-logic/23-audiobook-source.md.

Option A · “Inline switch”

The switch sits in the player sheet, under Voice.

For

  • One tap to switch.
  • Everything in one place.

Against

  • The caption line gets crowded.
  • Little room for the chapter-mode note and errors.
  • The segmented control is tight at 200 % font.

Option B · “Audio source sheet” Recommended

One list row in the player sheet opens a sheet with room for the details.

For

  • Room for status, chapter-mode and error text.
  • The sheet row stays one line.
  • Scales to a third source later (file 24’s computer helper shows as a status line, not a source).

Against

  • Two taps to switch.

Option C · “Source chip”

A small chip on the pill and in the Listen view toggles the source.

For

  • Fastest switch.
  • Always visible.

Against

  • An easy accidental toggle.
  • No place for errors or chapter mode.
  • The pill is already full at 200 % font.

Shared by all options

These screens are identical in A, B and C.
