Quiet v2: what changed from option 3

  1. No visible scrollbars anywhere. Every scrolling area keeps scrolling (finger, wheel, keyboard, scroll chaining), but no scrollbar is ever drawn: not in the phone screens, not on the mockup page around them.
  2. Library: option 1's layout in Quiet's style, with Quiet's "Open PDF" button. Option 1's library shows the current book as a Continue card with a big play button, then Recent rows with cover tiles and "Continue · 34 %".
  3. Reader and player: Quiet's reader plus a Listen view (option 4's stage), opened by tapping the pill. The Listen view is option 4's stage, restyled in Quiet tokens.
  4. Export: option 4's design, extended. Option 4 (like option 1) pushes an Export page inside the player sheet with a header and back arrow, instead of Quiet's inline expanding row.
  5. Lock screen and media notification: show the current sentence. The design: the sentence becomes the media title, updated per sentence (each sentence is already its own media item, so this costs nothing extra), and the chapter and book become the second line.

How to try

  1. Tap the play button on "The Long Book".
  2. Tap "Ch. 7 · p. 265" on the pill to open the Listen view.
  3. Tap the big text to pause; swipe it sideways to change sentence.
  4. Tap More → Export audiobook; change the speed and quality and watch the estimate.
  5. Tap the chevron (or ◁ Back) to return to the text at the same sentence.
  6. Scroll the text down to shrink the pill; drag the pill up for the player; long-press play for speed.
  7. Switch Theme to Black.
  8. Long-press "A Field Guide to Small Gardens" (or right-click it) → Remove from library → Remove; then tap Undo.
  9. Library ⚙ → Storage: tap a book, an exported audiobook, and "Clear audio cache".
  10. Choose "Phone space: Nearly full", then play → ⋯ → Export audiobook: see "Not enough space", tap "Export part of the book", change "To", and start it.
  11. On "Not enough space", tap "Free up space" and delete the two Field Guide audiobooks until the banner says there's room; go back.
  12. Start a whole-book export with "Plenty", then switch to "Nearly full" while it runs (out of space, parts kept).
  13. Tap "Android Settings ›" → "Clear storage" opens Lectrice's Storage screen.

Notes

  • E5b copy says "Free up about {needed − free}" (222 MB: the amount still missing), while the line under it says "Needs about 342 MB free · 120 MB free now". Every "Free up about …" on the page is the missing amount, never the total needed (user decision, 24 October).
  • The whole file is M4B-only (user decision, 24 October): the Format group, its "Spec change" badge and the MP3 notes were removed from E1, E1x, E2–E5 and the live phone; summaries read "Heart · Full quality · 1×", and only E4 / E4p / E4s name the format.
  • Clearing the audio cache doesn't raise "free". Free space is getAllocatableBytes, which already counts our cache (cacheDir) as free (file 10 §5). So in the live demo, "Clear audio cache" lowers Lectrice's total but not the export's shortfall; deleting exports or removing a book does. The banner number follows that rule.
  • Removing a book frees "about {library + cache} MB" even when its exports' checkbox is ticked (spec §3.1 defines n that way). The dialog doesn't re-count when the box is ticked.
  • "Save to Audiobooks" doesn't change the phone's free space (the file moves on the same phone); it lowers Lectrice's own total only. The live demo follows that.

Impact (what these choices cost)

  • Speed baked into the file is new for the core. Today audio is always made at 1× and the player changes speed (ExoPlayer's Sonic time-stretch on Android, the browser on the desktop). An export at 1.5× needs a time-stretch step in the export runner before each part is encoded. Proposed: Sonic's algorithm (Apache-2.0, the same one ExoPlayer uses, so the file sounds exactly like in-app listening) behind a small port in the core, so the desktop gets it too (it could also use ffmpeg atempo). The export record gains speed; chapter times are divided by the speed; resume must keep the same speed; start_export(…) gains speed and model. Cached 1× clips stay valid and can be reused. (Kokoro's own speed input would sound slightly more natural but can't reuse the cache; not chosen.)
  • Quality choice: the core holds one loaded engine. If the export uses the model you are not listening with, running both at once would need a second engine in memory (about +300 MB RAM for fp32, UNVERIFIED). Decision: in that case the export waits while you listen and continues when playback stops; the page says so.
