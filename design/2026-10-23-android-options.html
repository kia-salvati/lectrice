5. My recommendation

Option 1 "Pill" as the base, plus three borrowed pieces:

  1. From option 3: the sheet follows the finger when you drag the pill up (Telegram's smoothness, cheap in Compose), and the true-black dark theme as a setting (if you want it, Q3). Not the auto-collapsing pill: hiding the chapter label costs more than the ~100 dp it saves.
  2. From option 4: the "‹ Section / Section ›" jumps in the player sheet. The full Listen stage is a good v1.1 idea once you've lived with v1; it fits on top of option 1 without changing it.
  3. From option 2: nothing visual, but the platform rules (edge-to-edge, predictive back, Material widgets for slider, switch and sheet) apply to every option anyway.

Why: you called D "perfect", and option 1 keeps one product across web and phone with one token set; it is closest to the spec, so M2 has the least risk; the pill is now a standard Android pattern, so it won't feel foreign. The rest of the options are there so you can see what you'd be giving up.

6. Questions for you

  1. Which option (or which mix)? If a mix, which pieces from the others?
  2. Wallpaper colours (Material You): keep our own colours only (spec), or offer them as an opt-in setting? I'd say no.
  3. True-black dark theme for OLED (saves a little battery at night): add it as a third theme choice?
  4. Sleep timer: I suggest yes for v1 (Off, 15, 30, 45 min, 1 h, End of chapter). It's cheap and every listening app has it. OK?
  5. Covers: generated title tiles (no core change), or a picture of the PDF's first page (a small core change: render page 1 with pdfium)? The cover also shows on the lock screen.
  6. Pill status opens the chapters sheet (saves a button; my proposal), or keep a separate chapters button as in the spec? The status would show a small "⌃" hint.
  7. Lock screen without a seek bar: each media item is one sentence (~5 s), so a scrubber there means nothing. Hide it? (Proposed: yes.)
  8. Section jumps ("‹ Section / Section ›") in the player sheet: want them?
  9. Text size: only in Settings, or also an "Aa" control in the player sheet?
  10. Tablets/foldables: phone layout with the text column centred at max 640 dp for v1. OK?
  11. "Not now, just read" on the first-run screen (use the app without the voice): keep it?
